Simple ways to save water

Small daily actions can make a meaningful difference.

🔧 Fix leaks quickly

Report dripping taps and leaking pipes instead of ignoring them.

🪣 Use a bucket

Use a bucket instead of a running hose when washing vehicles or outdoor spaces.

🚿 Reduce shower time

Turn off the tap while brushing and avoid unnecessary water flow.

🌧️ Collect rainwater

Where safe and permitted, collect rainwater for gardening and cleaning.

🌿 Water plants wisely

Water plants early in the morning or evening to reduce evaporation.

📢 Spread awareness

Share water-saving habits with neighbours, friends and family.

About JalaRaksha

Goal: Encourage community participation in identifying water problems and reducing wastage.

Target users: Residents, farmers, local volunteers and Panchayat water-supply workers.

Proposed workflow: A resident submits a report → the community team reviews it → action status is updated → residents can see progress.

Technology used: HTML for structure, CSS for design and JavaScript for interactive features. Browser local storage keeps demo reports on this device.

Future improvements: GPS location tagging, photo uploads, multilingual Telugu/English interface, verified user accounts, SMS alerts and a real administrator dashboard.

Important limitation: This prototype is a front-end demonstration. It is not connected to live water sensors, a government database or an official complaint service.
